TS06 — Compact MLP, LSTM, GRU and causal TCN forecasting¶

Goal¶

Evaluate small neural networks with the same smoothed-input and target contracts as TS05. Measure variability across three fixed seeds, use chronological early stopping, and compare seed ensembles with baselines and frozen earlier procedures. Complexity must earn its place through the saved evaluation; no architecture is presumed superior.

Observed results¶

Four compact architectures were evaluated with three fixed seeds each and separate track selection. At five sessions, selected returns MSE skill is 0.0262 against zero over 30 scored origins. At five sessions, selected smoothed MSE skill is 0.1655 against filter_nochange over 30 scored origins. Seed variability is reported separately from historical sampling uncertainty. Results remain exploratory; no trading advantage is established.

Setup¶

Requires PyTorch >=2.5 and scikit-learn >=1.6, alongside the prior dependencies. All training uses CPU, one PyTorch thread and deterministic algorithms. Each fit starts from scratch. Exact results may differ across library versions or hardware even with the same seed. No model download, GPU or previous notebook execution is required.

Steps¶

1. Declare architectures, seeds and training budget¶

Each track has its own five-output network. The input is a 20-by-2 window of causal EMA increments; the smoothed track predicts residuals above its mechanical anchor, and the return track directly predicts actual cumulative log returns.

MLP flattens the lag window. LSTM/GRU use one unidirectional recurrent layer and the last hidden output. The causal TCN uses left padding and dilations 1,2,4,8, giving receptive field 31 observations (covering the 20-position window with zero left padding). No right-padding leakage, bidirectional state or future teacher forcing is used.

2. Purge the chronological stopping split¶

For each available training pool, reserve its last 20% of feature origins for stopping. Optimization-training examples must have their full five-session labels end before the first validation feature origin. Overlapping trailing input histories are allowed; unavailable future labels are not. Stopping-stage scalers use optimization rows only.

Choose the epoch count with minimum chronological validation loss within a maximum of 30 epochs and patience 5. Then reset the same seed, refit scalers on all mature examples, and train a fresh network for exactly that many epochs. This uses no future observation beyond the current forecast origin. The loss is mean MSE across individually standardized horizon targets; outer model selection still uses relative MAE.

dataset=SupervisedData(market_x,features)

3. Evaluate fixed seed ensembles chronologically¶

At every inner and outer origin, fit all four architectures for both tracks with all three seeds. Average the three predictions within each architecture; do not select the best seed. If any seed fails, the architecture uses the predeclared baseline fallback and records the incomplete ensemble. Individual seed results remain available.

Selection is separate by track and frozen within each outer block. The baseline is always eligible. The 30 scored outer origins and training budget match TS05.
